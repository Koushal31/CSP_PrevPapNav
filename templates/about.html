About PapersNav

Built by students, for students — making exam preparation easier across Tamil Nadu's engineering colleges.

Our Mission

Previous Papers Navigator was created to solve a simple problem: engineering students waste hours hunting for old question papers scattered across WhatsApp groups, Google Drive folders, and senior contacts.

We built one central place, organized by college, branch, semester, and subject — so you can spend time practicing instead of searching.
